ForeverMacros

Macro texts adapted from:
- Classic WoW Forever, https://classicwowforever.com/macros/ (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Changes: spell and item names are resolved from IDs in the client's language, unranked names are raised to the
  highest rank the character knows, some macros were shortened to stay within 255 characters in every language.
- Zockify, https://www.zockify.com/forever/druid/macros/ (druid ideas)
Spell, item and rank data: WoW Forever 1.60.1.70532 client tables (via wago.tools).
